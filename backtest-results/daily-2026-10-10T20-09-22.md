Capital Rs 10,00,000 in 10 equal slots, slippage 0.1% per side. Signals use the close, fills use the NEXT open.

## Read this before believing any of it

- The stock list is today's list, so stocks that fell out of favour are missing (survivorship bias). This flatters every strategy.
- Beating the Nifty matters: a strategy that makes less than just owning the Nifty, with more risk and more charges, is not worth running.
- Each slot is a fixed Rs 1,00,000 (profits are not compounded), so a very bad strategy can show a loss bigger than the starting capital. That is a sign to throw it away, not a real account outcome.
- A pass means "worth paper-trading", never "will make money". Charges are assumptions (see scripts/daily/engine.mjs), compare with your contract note.
